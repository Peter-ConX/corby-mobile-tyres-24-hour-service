Helpful Roadside Guide

HOW TO FIND YOUR TYRE SIZE

Tyre size information is embossed directly onto your tyre sidewall. Having this ready when you call ensures we bring the exact tyre for your vehicle.

Example Tyre Sidewall Marking:
205 / 55 R 16 91V

Note: This is an example to show format. Your vehicle's size will be on your tyre sidewall.

205 Width (mm)

The tyre width across the tread in millimetres.

55 Profile / Ratio (%)

The height of the tyre sidewall as a % of the width.

R 16 Rim Diameter

Radial construction fitting a 16-inch wheel rim.

91V Load & Speed

The weight capacity index and maximum speed rating.

💡

Can't see the size or stranded in the dark? Just call us with your vehicle registration, make, and model — we can help check compatibility.

STRANDED ROADSIDE SAFETY CHECKLIST

UK Emergency Breakdown Safety Procedure
1.

Move to a safe position: Pull as far left off the carriageway, hard shoulder, or verge as safely possible.

2.

Turn on hazard warning lights: Make your vehicle clearly visible to other drivers, especially at night or in rain.

3.

Exit away from live traffic: All passengers should exit via the nearside (passenger) doors and stand behind safety barriers if on a dual carriageway.

4.

Do not attempt dangerous repairs: Never attempt to change a tyre on the traffic side of a busy road. Wait for mobile assistance.

ROADSIDE TYRE ASSISTANCE FAQ

Answers to common questions when calling for mobile tyre assistance in Corby.

Do you provide 24-hour mobile tyre fitting in Corby?

Yes, we provide 24-hour emergency roadside and driveway tyre fitting assistance across Corby and surrounding Northamptonshire areas. When an unexpected flat or blowout occurs at night or on weekends, call 07404 355044.

What areas around Corby do you cover?

Our central base is located near Boden Close (NN18 9BT). We cover Corby Town Centre, Oakley Vale, Weldon, Priors Hall Park, Great Oakley, Cottingham, Little Stanion, Kettering corridor, and surrounding A43 and A427 routes.

Can you come to my home or workplace?

Yes. In addition to roadside emergencies, we frequently carry out tyre replacements and puncture repairs on driveways, private car parks, and workplace yards, so you don't have to waste time sitting in a tyre shop.

Can you repair my tyre instead of replacing it?

If the puncture is in the safe central tread area and the internal structure has not been damaged by driving on it flat, a safe repair can often be completed. If the puncture is on the sidewall, shoulder, or the tyre is shredded, a replacement tyre is legally and safely required.

What information should I have ready when calling?

Having three details helps us attend promptly: (1) Your tyre size (e.g. 205/55 R16), (2) Your exact location or postcode, and (3) Whether your car has locking wheel nut keys available in the boot or glovebox.

Do you offer both new and part-worn tyres?

Yes, our mobile service can supply both new tyres and quality part-worn options depending on what is available in stock for your specific tyre dimensions and budget.

How do I pay for mobile tyre service?

Payment is made upon completion of the service on-site. The technician will confirm the agreed callout and tyre price upfront before proceeding with the work.
