Frequently Asked Questions

Roadside Tyre Assistance FAQ

Do you operate 24 hours a day in Corby? bolt

Yes, we run an around-the-clock emergency roadside and driveway tyre fitting operation 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year across Corby and Northamptonshire.

Do you come to roadside locations or motorways? bolt

Yes, our mobile fitting vans are fully autonomous workshops fitted with on-board power generators, high-capacity jacks, bead breakers, and digital balancers to service you anywhere safe roadside, in laybys, car parks, or driveways.

Can you repair punctures at the roadside? bolt

Yes, if the puncture is localized in the central tread area compliant with British Standard BSAU159 guidelines. If the sidewall or shoulder is punctured, we carry replacement tyres to ensure your safety.

Can you replace tyres at my location? bolt

Yes, we carry a large inventory of tyres ranging from economical budget options to premium brand tyres. We replace the tyre, fit a new valve, digitally balance the wheel, and dispose of your old tyre safely.

What information should I provide when calling? bolt

Please have your tyre size ready (embossed on your tyre sidewall, such as 205/55 R16), your current vehicle location or postcode, and whether your car has locking wheel nut keys available.
